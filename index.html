About Warangal HealthRAG

The Warangal HealthRAG Clinical Information System is designed to address healthcare accessibility barriers in Warangal District, Telangana. By providing clear, grounded primary care advice in simple language, it guides patients on whether their symptoms can be managed at a local Primary Health Centre (PHC) or require escalation to MGM Hospital.
