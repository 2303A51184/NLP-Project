How Dual RAG Works

Four-step deterministic and semantic information retrieval pipeline.

1

Patient Input

User describes symptoms in simple English or everyday terms without clinical jargon.

2

Red-Flag Check

Safety rule engine screens for acute red flags (cardiac arrest, stroke, trauma) in <1 ms.

3

Dual Vector Search

TF-IDF and FAISS dense semantic embeddings retrieve top-K verified matching patient cases.

4

Grounded Output

Synthesizes clinical guidance strictly with hospital facility names and record IDs.
