Why This System Matters

Overcoming rural healthcare delays and eliminating dangerous AI hallucinations in Warangal.

1

Relieving Referral Centers

MGM Hospital Warangal treats thousands of outpatients daily. This platform helps patients assess whether symptoms can be managed at a local Primary Health Centre (PHC) or require hospital escalation.

2

Zero Hallucinations

Standard generative models routinely invent medical diagnoses or incorrect dosages. Our system mathematically bounds responses strictly to authenticated clinical records.

3

Evidence Transparency

Every answer features an expandable "Why am I seeing this answer?" section displaying the exact record ID, similarity score, and department.

How RAG Works

Four-step deterministic and semantic information retrieval pipeline.

A

1. Patient Input

User describes symptoms in simple words without medical jargon.

B

2. Red-Flag Check

Safety filter checks for acute red flags (chest pain, dyspnea, stroke signs).

C

3. Vector Search

TF-IDF and FAISS dense embeddings retrieve matching clinical cases in <35 ms.

D

4. Grounded Output

Synthesizes clear guidance with exact source tags (WP-SYN-XXXX).
