Why This System Matters in Warangal

Designed specifically to address healthcare crowding and misinformation in semi-urban and rural Telangana.

🏥

Relieving Referral Center Pressure

Regional centers like MGM Hospital Warangal receive over 3,000 outpatients daily. This platform helps patients assess whether their symptoms can be managed locally at a Primary Health Centre or require hospital escalation.

🛡️

Zero AI Hallucinations

Unlike unconstrained chatbots like ChatGPT which invent medical facts or incorrect medication names, our system is mathematically constrained to retrieve answers derived solely from authenticated clinical records.

🔍

Transparent Evidence Inspector

Patients and medical staff can click "Verify This Answer" under any response to inspect the exact dataset record ID, similarity match score, and grounded text chunk used to form the recommendation.

How Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Works

A multi-stage deterministic and semantic architecture delivering clinical reliability.

1

Patient Input

User queries symptoms in simple English or colloquial phrasing without needing technical clinical jargon.

2

Red-Flag Triage

Deterministic regex safety layer intercepts critical emergency keywords to alert emergency casualty units.

3

Vector Retrieval

Dual retrieval engine (TF-IDF sublinear search or FAISS MiniLM dense vectors) finds top-k matching clinical cases.

4

Grounded Guidance

Generates structured advice with source citation tags (WP-SYN-XXXX) and local hospital guidance.

Production-Ready Platform Features

Engineered to international healthcare SaaS standards with rigorous role-based security.

⚡

Sub-35 ms Latency

Optimized TF-IDF sparse matrix computation delivers instantaneous recommendations on standard CPU infrastructure without GPU requirements.

🔐

Role-Based Security

Integrated SQLite database with PBKDF2 password hashing, separating standard patient sessions from the private administrator dashboard (/admin).

☁️

Google Colab & VS Code Ready

Fully decoupled backend.py architecture allowing interactive notebook exploration in Google Colab and local VS Code development.
